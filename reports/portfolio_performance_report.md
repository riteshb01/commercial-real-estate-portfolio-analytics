## 4. Lease Rollover Stress Testing & Cash Flow Sensitivity

To evaluate portfolio resilience, the 2026–2027 expiring lease pool ($27.80M annual revenue) was stress-tested across 5 retention scenarios:

| Scenario | Tenant Renewal Rate | Retained Cash Flow ($M) | Cash Flow Loss ($M) | Resulting Portfolio NOI ($M) | Impact on NOI (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Case** | 100% Renewal | $27.80M | $0.00M | $42.64M | 0.0% |
| **Mild Downturn** | 80% Renewal | $22.24M | -$5.56M | $37.08M | -13.0% |
| **Moderate Stress** | 60% Renewal | $16.68M | -$11.12M | $31.52M | -26.1% |
| **Severe Stress** | 40% Renewal | $11.12M | -$16.68M | $25.96M | -39.1% |
| **Catastrophic Vacancy** | 0% Renewal | $0.00M | -$27.80M | $14.84M | -65.2% |

### Strategic Takeaway:
Even under the **Moderate Stress (60% Renewal)** scenario, the portfolio maintains **$31.52M in annual NOI** and generates positive debt service coverage.

## 5. Strategic Asset Management Recommendations

1. **Execute Early Anchor Renewals in Atlanta & Austin:**
   - Prioritize lease extension discussions with *FedEx Supply Chain* (Hartsfield Logistics) and *Apple Inc.* (Austin Tech Center).
2. **Capture Mark-to-Market Rent Upside:**
   - In-place rents in Industrial and Class A Office are currently **6.8% to 11.2% below prevailing market asking rents**, providing an estimated **$3.2M annual revenue upside** upon renewal.
3. **Implement OPEX Containment Program in Dallas-Fort Worth & Charlotte:**
   - Audit property tax assessments and renegotiate regional CAM vendor contracts to lower property operating expense ratios toward the 45% institutional target.
